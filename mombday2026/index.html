THE SHORT VERSION

How to play

  1. You start at your laptop. Roll dice, choose a green highlighted square, then press Move. Move up to the number you rolled, using doorways to get around walls.
  2. Land on Lunch, Laundry and Plants in any order. Watch Mom’s action, then press the button below it to finish the task. Passing over a target doesn’t count.
  3. The WFH police moves up to 2 squares toward you after each turn. Walls block both of you. Orange squares show where he can reach next; don’t walk onto him.
  4. Dad is optional: visit him for a pink heart shield that protects you from one catch and keeps your progress. He can help once per game.
  5. After all three tasks are checked off, the laptop’s circle starts pulsing. Land on it to win! Until then, the laptop isn’t a safe zone.
  6. Caught without a shield? You return to the laptop and all tasks reset.
